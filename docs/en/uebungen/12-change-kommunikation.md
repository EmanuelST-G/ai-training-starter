# Exercise 12: Plan Change Communication for an AI Copilot

[← back to exercises overview](index.md)

**Phase:** 3
**Duration:** 45 minutes
**Prerequisite:** [Change Management and AI Adoption](../projektleitung/11-change.md)
**Format:** Group work

## Task

A customer service copilot should deliver answer drafts. The team is worried about job cuts, performance monitoring and liability for errors. The pilot starts in six weeks with 30 volunteers.

Create a first announcement, a communication plan for six weeks, three FAQ answers and two measurable adoption goals. Research one current, serious practical reference on AI adoption or AI literacy and document source, date and transfer limit.

## Hints

- Communicate benefits and limits at the same time.
- Do not promise anything the project lead cannot guarantee.
- Name feedback, escalation and co-design opportunities.

## Solution

A credible first announcement reads:

> In six weeks 30 volunteers test a copilot that creates answer drafts based on approved content. It does not send anything itself; the subject-matter decision stays with the service employees. The goal is to reduce search and phrasing time, not to evaluate individual performance. We openly measure quality, time saving and workload. Errors and critical cases can be reported without personal disadvantage. An expansion is decided by business unit, data protection, works council and pilot group only after the evaluation.

| Time | Measure | Audience and owner | Success signal |
|------|---------|---------------------|----------------|
| Week −6 | Announcement and open Q&A | whole team, department lead | Questions documented |
| Week −5 | Process demo with error examples | team, product owner | Limits understood |
| Week −4 | Workshop with works council and data protection | interest representation, project lead | Open items with owner |
| Week −3 | Select volunteers and measure baseline | pilot group, eval lead | Baseline time and quality available |
| Week −2 | Role training and practising safe inputs | pilot group, change lead | Practical test passed |
| Week −1 | Rehearse support channel and stop rule | pilot group, service owner | Test report processed |
| Week +2 | Pulse survey and office hour | pilot group | Measures from feedback |

**FAQ 1: Does the copilot replace jobs?** The pilot evaluates a work process, not a personnel measure. Statements on long-term roles are made by the department lead only in the designated co-determination processes.

**FAQ 2: Are my inputs used for performance evaluation?** Only the data agreed for quality and operations is collected. Purpose, access and retention are transparently defined with data protection and works council.

**FAQ 3: Who is liable for a wrong answer?** The draft must be reviewed before sending. Errors are reported via the support channel; the organisation clarifies responsibility and improves the system and process instead of routinely assigning blame to individuals.

Adoption goals are at least 80 % weekly active pilot people from week two and at least 70 % of drafts rated helpful, without rising correction errors. The external practical reference is only adopted when method, target group and context fit; the research entry names primary source, retrieval date and explicitly states why the result is not directly transferable to this team.

For the evaluation usage numbers are reported only at group level. Free-text feedback is bundled by topic, given a responsible role and answered within an announced deadline.

## Reflection

Change communication earns trust through verifiable statements, participation and a visible handling of errors. Project managers must run technical introduction, co-determination and learning loops as one shared workflow.
